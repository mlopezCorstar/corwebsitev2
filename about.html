About Corstar Communications

Our Story

Corstar Communications LLC was founded in 1970 in Westchester County, NY. We started by helping local businesses manage their communications infrastructure. Back when that meant PBX telephone system rooms and COAXial cables. Over the decades, moving from Dial Up to DSL to Broadband, and from Mainframes to Servers to the Cloud. Now we have landed as full-service IT MSP, with compliance and security specialists.

Yet we haven't changed our core beliefs:

  1. Technology should work for your business, not the other way around.
  2. Your IT should not be a mystery in a basement closet.
  3. We are your technology partners, an extension of your team.

Our longevity in the IT market speaks to our commitment to customer satisfaction. We've adapted and grown alongside technological advances, even now as AI changes the game. We are always staying ahead of the curve to provide our clients with efficient solutions to complex problems.

Today, we're proud to be a trusted IT partner for businesses of all sizes (including schools, municipalities, and non-profits) across New York, New Jersey, and Connecticut.

1970
Founded in Westchester County

Started as a telecommunications and cabling company serving local businesses.

1980s
Early Computing Infrastructure

Expanded into small business computing as PCs entered the workplace.

1990s
Network & Internet

Began connecting Westchester businesses, non-profits, and municipalities to the internet for the first time.

Early 2000s
IT Leadership Joins the Team

Our Premier Director of IT, Alex Simeoforides, joins the team.

2010s
The Cloud Arrives

Email (Microsoft 365/Gmail) and software Cloud adoption start taking off. These migrations need a new level of cross-departament technology planning, and we stood up to the task.

2017
Cybersecurity Gets Serious

The start of the modern Ransomware Attack. Recruited new, security trained, team members. They are tasked to protect client data, recover from disaster, and train client staff.

2020
Full-Service IT MSP

Along with the work changes of the pandemic, Corstar adapted to provide all of its clients the ability to quickly move to work from home setups. We updated our services to match the modern "everywhere, anytime" state of businesses.

Today
AI Consulting & Compliance

AI presents new workflow improvements and capabilities. IT also brings new security and compliance concerns. Many businesses still are not sure what it is or if they are using it.

We have stepped up to the plate by getting trained and certified as OpenAI and Claude Partners. Offering agentic engineering support.
