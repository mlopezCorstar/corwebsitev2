Remote Office Support

Give employees outside the main office a reliable way to get help, stay connected, and keep working securely.

Talk To Our Experts
Support Without the Wait

Keep Distributed Teams Productive

Corstar helps remote and satellite-office employees resolve common technology problems without waiting for an on-site visit. With an authorized remote session, our engineers can diagnose many issues directly and guide the user through the solution.

We also help standardize devices, software, access, and documentation so employees receive a consistent experience wherever they work.

What We Support

  • Remote troubleshooting for computers and business software
  • Microsoft 365, email, and collaboration tools
  • Secure access to company systems and files
  • Device setup, updates, and user onboarding
  • Home and satellite-office connectivity guidance
  • Escalation when an on-site visit is required
