Network Assesment and Optimization

Find the bottlenecks, coverage gaps, and configuration issues that slow your team down, then put a practical improvement plan in place.

Schedule a Network Assesment
See the Whole Network

Turn Network Friction into a Clear Action Plan

Corstar reviews how your network is designed, configured, documented, and experienced by users. We identify the changes that will make the biggest difference to reliability and performance.

The result is a prioritized roadmap—not a list of products you do not need.

What We Review

  • Internet, switching, routing, and firewall performance
  • Wireless coverage, capacity, and interference
  • Network diagrams, configurations, and documentation
  • Bandwidth use, bottlenecks, and recurring connectivity issues
  • Redundancy, failover, and growth requirements
  • Prioritized recommendations and implementation options
