AI Deployment & Management

Your team is already using AI. We put that usage inside a governed environment — with visibility, access controls, and your data staying where it belongs.

Talk To Our Experts
The Problem

Your Team's Already Using AI. Are You Sure It's Secure?

People in every department are using AI to draft documents, summarize meetings, analyze spreadsheets, and speed up routine work. Most of it happens on personal accounts, through tools nobody approved, with no record of what was shared.

That is where the real exposure sits. Uncontrolled AI does not only create risk — it removes accountability. Once a client file, a financial model, or a signed contract has been pasted into a consumer chatbot, there is no log, no retention policy, and no answer for the auditor who asks where that information went.

Corstar brings the usage into the open, moves it into an environment you actually control, and keeps it aligned with the security and compliance obligations you already carry.

🔦

Shadow AI, Everywhere

Free accounts and browser extensions spread faster than policy does. Most owners are surprised by how many AI tools are already in daily use.

📄

Data You Can't Trace

Consumer tools keep no audit trail you can produce. If regulated data leaves the building, you find out afterwards, if at all.

🧠

Real Value, Left on the Table

Banning AI outright pushes it underground. Deployed deliberately, the same tools cut hours out of finance, sales, and admin work every week.
